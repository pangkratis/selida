01Overview

Selida is a book discovery and reading-tracker app. To do its job — tracking what you're reading, timing your sessions, and recommending books you'll like — it needs to store some information about your account and your reading activity. This policy covers exactly what that is, why we keep it, and what you can do about it.

In short

We collect your account details and your own reading activity. We don't run ads, don't use analytics or tracking SDKs, and don't sell data to anyone.

02Information we collect

We collect information in three categories, all tied to your account:

CategoryWhat it includes
Account information Email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider — we never see it in plain text), display name, preferred language, and country/catalog preference.
Reading activity Books you add to your reading list, their status (wishlist, reading, completed), reading progress, and reading session timestamps and durations.
App activity Actions like viewing a book's details or adding it to your wishlist, used to power recommendations. Optional feedback you choose to submit through the app.

We do not request access to your camera, photo library, contacts, or location — Selida doesn't use any of them.

03How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account, and keep you signed in.
  • To track your reading progress, timed sessions, and reading history across your devices.
  • To personalize book recommendations based on what you've read, saved, or shown interest in.
  • To respond to feedback you submit through the app.
  • To keep the service reliable and secure.

04Where your data lives

Your data is stored with Supabase, our backend and database provider, hosted on infrastructure in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden). Access to your data is restricted so that only you can read or modify your own account, reading list, and activity — this is enforced at the database level, not just in the app.

05Third-party services

Selida works with a small, deliberately short list of outside services:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend hosting, as described above.
  • Biblionet — a Greek book-catalog data source. Selida queries Biblionet to look up book information (titles, authors, descriptions, subjects); this is a one-way lookup of public catalog data — your personal account information and reading activity are never sent to Biblionet.
In short

No analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no crash-reporting trackers, no social-login data sharing. Selida doesn't include any of them.

06Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Your reading activity and account details are used only to operate Selida itself.

07Your rights & controls

  • Access and update your display name, language, and country preference at any time from Settings.
  • Delete your account directly in the app — Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, reading list, reading sessions, and activity history. It cannot be undone.
  • Export or ask about your data by contacting us (below) if you'd like a copy of what we hold.
In short

Deleting your account in Settings removes everything — there's no separate request process needed.

08Data retention

We keep your account and reading data for as long as your account exists, so that your reading history and recommendations stay intact between sessions. When you delete your account, your data is removed immediately and permanently.

09Children's privacy

Selida is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

10International transfers

Selida's infrastructure is based in the European Union. If you use Selida from outside the EU, your information will be transferred to and processed in the EU, under the safeguards our infrastructure provider maintains.

11Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Selida after a change means you accept the update.
